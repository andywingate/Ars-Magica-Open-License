# Aldric of Mercere

*The Iron Line — a Gifted Mercere warrior-magus for the road north*
*Ars Magica 5th Edition*

---

## Concept

Aldric is a big, weather-beaten Bohemian, a Gifted magus of House Mercere who was a fighting man
before he was ever a wizard. He does not throw fire. He walks down the shield wall with his hands
out, and the men he touches become **better**. Not braver, not luckier. Physically better: flesh
that turns a blade for the length of a melee, and a wounded man who fights on as though the wound
were not there.

He needs nothing to do it. No vis, no Ritual, no season in a laboratory — every spell he owns casts
free, in armour, in the field, as often as the day demands.

He is no bystander when it starts. He fights in chain mail with sword and shield, he rides, and
he leads four men in the line himself. His Gift still unsettles people, as every magus's does —
but not horses, and not the arithmetic of a shield wall.

His limits define him as sharply as his gifts. He cannot destroy anything by magic: Perdo is alien
to him and every Perdo total is halved. He has no attack spell and no Penetration, so anything
with Magic Resistance is simply beyond his reach. He cannot make anyone hit harder — only harder
to kill. And he has never yet accepted that a man under his command might have to be left behind.

## Appearance and Background

He stands a head taller than most men, broad through the shoulder, with the scarred forearms of
someone who learned the sword before he learned his letters. Fair hair worn short. His eyes are a
pale, unsettling amber.

Born in Prague to a minor Bohemian family that held its land from a greater one, he was put to the
sword young and was already in his lord's household troop at fourteen, in the years when King
Ottokar's knights were riding north and east to the Emperor's wars. It was there, at fifteen, that
Constance of Mercere found him — a travelling maga and a healer working the roads that carry the
Order's messages between Prague and the Rhine.

She took him as an apprentice late, and shaped his Arts around the body and its perfection. He
turned that training somewhere she had not intended: where Constance mended the sick, Aldric
hardens the whole. He came out of his Gauntlet at thirty this year, knowing seven spells and
nothing at all about the politics of the Order — and House Mercere is already quietly noting down
everything he owes it. His first posting is north, to the frontier beyond the Empire's edge, where
the fighting is and where nobody has met him before.

He speaks Czech and the Low German of the soldiery and the Baltic trade, which between them carry
him from Prague to Riga.
