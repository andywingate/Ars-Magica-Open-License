# Aldric of Mercere — Tactics and Development

The wider picture: the constraints his magic works under, the combat rules worth knowing, and
where his experience should go. Core rules only.

---

## What kind of magus this is

He is a **specialist with a very short reach**. Two Arts, four spells, one of them a Ritual he can
cast a few times a year at most. His entire magical contribution is:

- **One man made permanently better**, at nine pawns of vis and a season's decision.
- **One man made temporarily better**, for twenty combat rounds, at a Fatigue level.
- **Wounded men who recover**, at +12 to their Recovery rolls between spell and Chirurgy.

That is all. He cannot attack, cannot ward, cannot scry, cannot fly, cannot make anything harder
to kill. Anything with Magic Resistance is simply outside his reach — he has no Penetration
Ability at all, deliberately, because a magus with no offensive magic has nothing to penetrate
with.

The other half of the character is a competent soldier with a longsword, a horse, and enough
Leadership to run four men — and the Gentle Gift, which is what makes the soldier half possible.

## The constraints his magic works under

**One man at a time.** A Group version of either Champion spell is two magnitudes dearer: level 55
and 60 against a Lab Total of 49. Both are out of reach and will stay out of reach for years. This
is the direct consequence of a Minor Focus that covers only the body's Characteristics, and it is
the honest shape of the concept.

**Ninth magnitude Warps whoever he touches.** *"Any Hermetic spell of sixth magnitude or
higher"* is a powerful supernatural effect, and level 45 is ninth. Anyone he casts a Champion
spell on gains a Warping Point — Score 1 after five points, Score 2 after fifteen. The only escape
is written into the rules: a version *"specially and carefully designed"* for one named man Warps
neither that man nor the caster. So each of his champions needs his own tailored version of the
spell, which is a season's work and forty-five levels each.

That constraint is a feature. It means he cannot mass-produce heroes, and every man he chooses is
a story.

**Touch range cannot reach himself.** *"Spells cast with any other Range, even if cast by the maga
on herself, do have to overcome the caster's own Magic Resistance."* His is Corpus 12 + five times
Parma 1 = **17**, and his Penetration is zero. So *The Champion's Hour* is for other people, full
stop. The Ritual he can self-cast at leisure with his Parma down, then raise it again.

**Encumbrance is a spellcasting statistic.** Casting Score is Technique + Form + Stamina −
Encumbrance + Aura, and Encumbrance is Burden minus Strength. In harness he is at Encumbrance 2,
which costs him two on every cast and two on Initiative — but **nothing on Attack or Defense**,
because *"in combat, Attack and Defense are not [penalised], as long as the Encumbrance is largely
due to weapons and armor."*

That makes **Strength a casting statistic for him**, which matters below.

## Combat rules worth knowing

- **Attack Advantage is added to the Damage Total.** A point of Attack is also a point of damage
  on every blow that lands — which is why raising a champion's Dexterity is worth more than it
  looks, and why a group, which inflicts its damage once per member, multiplies the effect.
- **Melee Soak takes no die.** Flat and reliable, unlike attack rolls.
- **A botched Defense roll sets the total to zero**, handing the attacker an Attack Advantage
  equal to their entire Attack Total. Usually fatal, and at Soak 11 it is very nearly the only way
  an ordinary soldier kills him.
- **Mounted combat adds the Ride score, up to +3, to Attack and Defense.** At Ride 3 that is the
  single cheapest bonus he owns — as much as Exertion, for free, all fight. The Gentle Gift is
  what lets him have a horse that will stand still.
- **Exertion** costs one Fatigue level for a bonus equal to his Combat Ability: **+7**, since
  Puissant adds to the Ability's value whenever it is used. On defence it covers every roll until
  his next turn.
- **Against missiles, only a shield's Defense bonus counts.** The heater shield's +3 is his whole
  equipment defence at range.
- **Wound penalties never apply to Soak.** A badly wounded Aldric is as hard to hurt as a fresh
  one; it is his Attack, Defense and casting that collapse.
- **A trained group** coordinates fighters equal to the leader's Leadership, with the other
  members' bonus capped at three times it. Leadership 4 is four men and up to +12.
- **Shield grogs**: a defended character *"is only injured if the defenders botch or are
  incapacitated"*, and the number defended may not exceed the number defending. His Driven Flaw
  means he will keep putting himself in front of them instead.

## Where his experience should go

**Parma Magica 2, then 3.** He has nine points banked toward the second level already. Parma 1 is
where every magus starts and where none should stay; his Magic Resistance against anything that is
not Corpus is 5, which is nothing.

**Concentration.** At 3 he holds a spell through being jostled 90% of the time and through being
wounded 38%. Every point is a point of reliability in exactly the situations he chooses to stand in.

**Creo and Corpus.** They are the whole character. Every point in either raises his Casting Score
twice over while the Form is the lower of the two, because the focus adds the lower Art again.
Corpus is currently the lower at 12, so **Corpus is worth double until it passes Creo 16.**

**Philosophiae and Artes Liberales.** Both add to every Ritual casting total, and his signature
spell is a Ritual. Cheap, permanent, and they compound with vis.

**Then the ritual he casts on himself.** *Blessing of the Steadfast Champion* raises a physical
Characteristic to no more than +2, and a magus takes no Warping from an effect he cast himself:

> **Strength +1 → +2** drops his Encumbrance from 2 to 1 and gives him **+1 damage, +1 Casting
> Score and +1 Initiative** at once. Nothing else in the game does that in one purchase.

**Longer term**, with a Creo Corpus Lab Total of 49, the base 45 guideline — raise a physical
Characteristic to no more than +3 — is level 50 at Touch range and one point out of reach. Magic
Theory 5, or Corpus 13, opens it. That is a genuine career goal: the magus who can make a man
better than any man has a right to be.

## Variant builds

If the troupe wants the same concept shaped differently:

| Change | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Tough instead of the Gentle Gift** | Soak 11 → 14, and his chance of being wounded by a man-at-arms falls from 6.1% to 3.8%. In exchange he takes −3 on every social interaction with mundanes, including Leadership, and Ride becomes hard to justify. This is the single biggest lever on the sheet |
| **Fast Caster** *(1 point)* | +3 Initiative on casting rounds, which would take his casting Initiative from −1 to +2 — better than his sword. Cheap, and the obvious thing to buy if a point comes free |
| **Method Caster** *(1 point)* | +3 to every Formulaic and Ritual Casting Total, which would make *The Champion's Hour* free in the field. Lost if he varies his gestures or voicing |
| **Skilled Parens** *(1 point)* | An extra 60 experience points and 30 spell levels during apprenticeship — the best raw value of any Minor Virtue at creation, and 30 more levels would buy back the Rego and healing spells this build had to drop |
| **Affinity with Single Weapon** *(1 point)* | Turns Warrior's 50 points into 75, and lets him exceed the age-based Ability cap by two |
| **A wider Focus** | If the storyguide would allow *bodily enhancement* rather than *the body's Characteristics*, he could add Muto Corpus Soak buffs and a group attack buff — but that is the version that was called too broad, and the narrow one makes a better character |

**Great (Characteristic)** looks tempting and is unavailable: it requires the score to be +3
already, so it cannot lift Strength from +1. The Ritual can.
